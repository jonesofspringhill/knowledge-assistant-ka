# Facilities Register

## Main community hall

The main community hall has a licensed seated capacity of 180 people. Its
standing layout is not approved for public events. The hall has a hearing loop,
an accessible entrance, and two marked emergency exits.

## Cedar meeting room

The Cedar meeting room has a maximum capacity of 24 people. The Facilities
Register is the authoritative source for room capacities and accessibility
features. Bookings must use the lower of the room limit and any event-specific
safety limit.
