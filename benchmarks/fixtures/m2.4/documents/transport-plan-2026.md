# Neighbourhood Transport Plan 2026

## Community bus pilot

The original community bus pilot began on 6 June 2026. It ran every 60 minutes
between the library and the health centre on weekdays. This document is the
historical launch plan and must not be treated as the current timetable after a
published replacement takes effect.

## Volunteer drivers

Drivers must be at least 21 years old, hold the required licence, and complete
the project driving check described in the Volunteer Handbook.
