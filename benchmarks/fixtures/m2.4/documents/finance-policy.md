# Community Finance Policy

## Matched funding and sponsorship

The council can match an approved external grant with up to GBP 2,500 from its
local participation fund. Match funding requires written confirmation of the
external award. A separate local-business sponsorship has a maximum value of
GBP 4,000 per project and cannot be counted as the applicant's own cash
contribution.

## Review cycle

The finance policy was adopted on 1 April 2025. Its scheduled review date was
originally 31 March 2026. Amendment F-7 replaced that date with 31 March 2027;
the later date is the current review deadline.
