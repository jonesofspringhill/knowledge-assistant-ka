# Community Funding Guide

## Grant limits

The Community Renewal Grant pays up to GBP 10,000 for one approved project. An
award is not automatic: the assessment panel scores eligibility, evidence of
need, delivery readiness, and value for money. Applicants must contribute at
least ten percent of the eligible project cost from another confirmed source.

## Dates and eligibility

Applications for the 2027 programme close at 17:00 on 30 September 2027. An
eligible applicant is a registered charity, community-interest company, or
unincorporated community group with a written constitution. Commercial firms
and individuals cannot apply directly. Decisions are issued by 15 December
2027, and funded work must start after the award letter.
