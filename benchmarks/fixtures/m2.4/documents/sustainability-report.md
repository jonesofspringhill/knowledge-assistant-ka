# Sustainability Report

## Solar installation

The community-centre solar array is rated at 42 kilowatts peak. During its first
complete reporting year it generated 38 megawatt-hours. The independently
modelled annual electricity-cost saving is GBP 6,200 at the tariff used in this
report.

## Insulation work

Roof and wall insulation reduced modelled annual heating demand by 18 percent.
The corresponding modelled annual gas-cost saving is GBP 4,800. Solar therefore
has the larger stated annual cost saving by GBP 1,400, while the measures affect
different energy uses.
