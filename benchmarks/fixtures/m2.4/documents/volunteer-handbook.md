# Volunteer Handbook

## Joining and safeguarding

General community volunteers must be at least 16 years old. Volunteers under 18
work with an adult supervisor and do not handle cash alone. Driving a project
vehicle is restricted to volunteers aged 21 or older who have completed the
approved driving check.

## Emergency contact procedure

The Volunteer Handbook is the primary document for emergency contact procedure.
During an incident, call the duty coordinator through the internal rota, give
the site name, and state whether emergency services have been contacted. The
handbook deliberately contains no private telephone number; the rota is held in
the local operational system.
